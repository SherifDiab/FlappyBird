# Privacy Policy for Flappy Bird

**Last Updated:** December 26, 2024

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Flappy Bird ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

### Information Collected Automatically

When you use our App, we may collect certain information automatically, including:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** How you interact with the App, game scores, session duration
- **Advertising Data:** Ad interactions, impressions, and clicks

### Information Collected by Third Parties

Our App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

#### Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in our App. AdMob may collect and process the following data:

- Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
- IP address
- Device information (model, OS version)
- Ad interaction data
- General location data (country/region level)

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain our App
- To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements
- To analyze usage patterns and improve our App
- To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

## Advertising

Our App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests or non-personalized based on contextual information.

### Ad Personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:

- **Android:** Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **iOS:** Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > Turn off Personalized Ads

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

- **Advertising Partners:** Google AdMob for ad serving purposes
- **Analytics Providers:** To help us understand App usage
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect our rights

## Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to provide the services described in this policy. Advertising data is managed according to Google's data retention policies.

## Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.
